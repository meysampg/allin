Why calling works here
Step 3 / 5
✓ Good call+1.8 big blinds on average
ANIMATION: the pot splits into 4 slices; your 50 fills 1 slice → "you pay 1 part to win 4, so you need to win more than 1 in 5 times (20%)"
ANIMATION: 9 heart cards light up in the deck → "9 outs × ~2% per card ≈ 18–19% next card, ~35% by the river"
The thinking, not the rule: compare what you pay to what you can win, then ask how often you win. You'll use this exact question in every spot from here on.
